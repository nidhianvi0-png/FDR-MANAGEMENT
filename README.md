# AICTE SMART FDR COMPLIANCE & INTELLIGENCE PORTAL

### An AI-Powered Platform for Intelligent FDR Management, Automated Verification and Compliance Monitoring

---

## 1. INTRODUCTION

The AICTE Smart FDR Compliance & Intelligence Portal is a proposed AI-powered digital platform designed to automate and simplify the management of Fixed Deposit Receipts (FDRs) submitted by educational institutions.

The platform aims to provide a centralized system for managing the complete FDR lifecycle, including document submission, data extraction, validation, officer review, approval, expiry monitoring, renewal and release requests.

By integrating Artificial Intelligence, Optical Character Recognition (OCR), cloud technologies and database management, the proposed solution aims to reduce repetitive administrative work and improve the efficiency of FDR compliance monitoring.

The portal will connect educational institutions and AICTE officers through a common digital platform, providing better visibility, organized records and intelligent decision-support capabilities.

**Project Vision:** To transform traditional FDR management into an intelligent, automated and transparent compliance management ecosystem.

---

## 2. PROBLEM STATEMENT

AICTE manages FDR-related records and compliance requirements associated with educational institutions. Managing multiple FDR documents through manual processes can create administrative challenges.

Traditional workflows involving emails, spreadsheets and PDF documents may lead to:

- Difficulty in managing a large number of FDR records.
- Time-consuming manual verification of documents.
- Delays in identifying incorrect or missing information.
- Missed FDR expiry dates.
- Delayed renewal procedures.
- Difficulty in tracking application status.
- Repetitive communication between institutions and officers.
- Limited centralized visibility into compliance-related activities.
- Increased administrative workload.

There is a need for an intelligent centralized platform that can automate document processing, improve monitoring and support officers in managing FDR-related activities.

## 3. PROPOSED SOLUTION

We propose an AI-powered FDR Compliance and Intelligence Portal that digitizes and automates important stages of the FDR management process.

The system will allow educational institutions to upload their FDR documents through an online portal. AI-powered document processing will extract important information, while automated validation will identify missing or potentially inconsistent details.

AICTE officers will be able to review submitted applications, verify extracted information, approve or reject requests and monitor FDR expiry and renewal activities through a centralized dashboard.

## 6. COMPLETE SYSTEM WORKFLOW

The proposed system follows a structured FDR lifecycle.

**STEP 1 – Institution Login**

The institution logs into the portal using its authorized credentials.

**STEP 2 – FDR Document Upload**

The institution uploads its FDR PDF document through the portal.

**STEP 3 – AI-Based OCR Extraction**

The document processing system extracts relevant information from the uploaded FDR document.

**STEP 4 – Automated Validation**

Extracted information is checked against predefined validation rules to identify missing or inconsistent details.

**STEP 5 – AICTE Officer Review**

The application and its extracted information become available on the officer dashboard.

**STEP 6 – Approval or Rejection**

The authorized officer reviews the application and records the appropriate decision.

**STEP 7 – Expiry Monitoring**

Approved FDR records are monitored for upcoming expiry dates and renewal requirements.

**STEP 8 – Renewal Management**

The system provides alerts to support timely renewal-related action.

**STEP 9 – FDR Release Request**

Institutions can submit release requests for review and further processing by authorized officers.

## 8. DASHBOARD AND ANALYTICS

The portal will provide a centralized dashboard for monitoring FDR-related activities.

### Key Dashboard Metrics

- **Total FDRs:** Total number of FDR records maintained in the system.
- **Pending Reviews:** Applications waiting for officer verification.
- **Expiring Soon:** FDRs approaching their expiry dates.
- **Renewal Pending:** Records requiring renewal-related action.
- **Release Requests:** FDR release applications awaiting review.
- **High-Risk Cases:** Records requiring additional attention based on defined compliance and risk indicators.

The dashboard will provide a quick overview of the current FDR management status and help officers prioritize important activities.

## 9. PROPOSED SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

The proposed architecture consists of five major layers.

### Layer 1 – User Interface

React.js / Next.js will provide the interface for institution users, AICTE officers and administrators.

### Layer 2 – Application Backend

Node.js will handle application requests, user operations, document workflows and communication between the frontend and database.

### Layer 3 – AI Processing Layer

Azure Document Intelligence will support document data extraction.

Azure OpenAI will support AI-generated summaries and the natural-language compliance copilot.

Azure AI Search will support information retrieval.

### Layer 4 – Database Layer

PostgreSQL will maintain structured information, including institution records, FDR details, expiry dates and application status.

### Layer 5 – Cloud Storage

Azure Blob Storage will store uploaded FDR PDF documents for future access and verification.
